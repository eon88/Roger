The platform

Behind every letting, a clearer workflow.

An agent workspace connects four role-specific portals. Roger records the next action and helps keep property information, requests and decisions in one place.

Enquiries to onboarding

Capture tenant, landlord and contractor prospects. Track reviews, next steps, applications and account setup.

Repairs and approvals

Record reports, contractor quotes, landlord approval decisions, visits, evidence and invoice reviews.

Separate role portals

Give tenants, landlords and tradespeople relevant views instead of access to the agent's full workspace.
